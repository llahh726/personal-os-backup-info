Privacy policy

Personal OS Encrypted Backups is a private desktop utility operated by its sole owner. Effective September 20, 2026.

Data and purpose

The utility uses Google Drive only to store and retrieve the owner's encrypted backups. It uses the drive.file permission for files created by, or explicitly opened with, this app. Account identity and storage quota are checked to confirm the intended backup destination.

Storage and security

Backup contents and the filenames within the restic repository are encrypted locally before upload. Google can still see the Google account, repository folder structure, encrypted object sizes and transfer activity. Google handles its service data under its own privacy policy.

OAuth credentials remain in protected local files. The outer recovery password is kept separately from the cloud backup. Anyone who obtains both the password and repository can decrypt the backup; encryption does not prevent deletion by someone controlling the Google account.

Use and sharing

Data accessed through Google APIs is used only for this backup and recovery function. It is not sold, used for advertising, shared with other users, or used to train machine-learning models. There is no separate operator-hosted data service or website analytics.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

The owner can revoke this app's access in their Google Account connections settings, remove local credentials, or delete its backup repository in Google Drive. Revocation stops access but does not itself erase existing backups. Copies persist until the owner deletes them, subject to Google Drive's trash and retention behavior.

Contact

For questions, use the app owner's support email displayed on the Google consent screen. This utility is for its owner's use only.
